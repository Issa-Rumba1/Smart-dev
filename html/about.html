My Story

The Story Behind SMART DEV

I am Issa Nyamawi Rumba, the founder of SMART DEV — a technology and digital solutions brand dedicated to providing practical, reliable and innovative IT solutions.

With a Diploma in Computer Science from the National Youth Service Camp Engineering Institute, I have built expertise across computer hardware maintenance, networking, database management, web development, graphic design and general IT support.

My goal is to leverage technology to solve real-world problems, empower businesses and bridge the digital divide. I am passionate about continuous learning and staying updated with the latest developments in the technology industry.

SMART DEV is my commitment to delivering quality technology services that make a genuine difference.
